## Description

Confused by parking signs? CanIPark reads them for you.

Simply point your iPhone camera at a parking sign, and CanIPark will instantly tell you:

- Whether you can park here right now
- How long you can stay
- Whether you need to pay
- When the rules change next

HOW IT WORKS

1. Open CanIPark and point your camera at the parking sign
2. The app reads the sign text using on-device text recognition
3. You get a clear, colour-coded answer in seconds

FEATURES

- Instant sign reading with on-device OCR
- Works offline, no internet connection needed
- Supports all standard Australian parking sign types
- Understands time windows, day ranges, and special conditions
- Detects payment requirements (meter, ticket, app)
- Handles public holiday exceptions
- School day awareness for school zone signs
- Recognises exemptions for buses, taxis, trucks, and permit holders
- Scan history so you can review past results
- Confidence indicator so you know how reliable the reading is

SUPPORTED SIGNS

CanIPark understands timed parking (1P, 2P, 4P, etc.), no stopping, no parking, clearways, loading zones, bus zones, taxi zones, truck zones, permit zones, accessible parking, EV charging spots, and more.

IMPORTANT

CanIPark is a convenience tool. Always read the actual sign before making your parking decision. The app may not correctly interpret damaged, unusual, or partially obscured signs. See our in-app disclaimer for full details.

Designed and built for Australian parking signs and road rules.

## Privacy

CanIPark processes all images on your device. No images or location data are sent to external servers. Camera access is used solely to photograph parking signs. Photo library access allows you to scan previously saved sign photos. Location data, if permitted, is used only to assist with local rule identification.
