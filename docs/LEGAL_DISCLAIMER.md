## Your Responsibility

You are solely responsible for your parking decisions. Before parking your vehicle, you should:

- Read the physical parking sign yourself
- Check for any additional signs on the same pole or nearby
- Look for temporary restrictions, council notices, or event-related changes
- Verify the current time and day
- Confirm whether the current day is a public holiday in your jurisdiction
- Ensure your vehicle type is permitted
- Pay any required parking fees

## Accuracy Limitations

CanIPark may produce incorrect results due to:

- Poor image quality, lighting, or camera angle
- Damaged, faded, or partially obscured signs
- OCR (optical character recognition) errors
- Unusual sign formats not covered by the parser
- Incorrect device clock or time zone settings
- State or council-specific rules not captured in the app's logic
- Signs with symbolic or pictographic content that cannot be read as text
- Temporary sign changes not reflected in the captured image
